[First run](@/guide/getting-started/first-run.md) from model import to a

lists the controls for Windows, Linux and macOS.

For a particular job, [Common workflows](@/guide/getting-started/workflows.md)
gives recipes for facing, squaring, pockets, holes, turning and re-posting.
